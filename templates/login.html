Privacy Policy
How we collect, use, and protect your data
Last updated March 2025 • SADAK AI is committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Data We Collect

Identity: Name, email, mobile. Location: GPS coordinates, state, district, village. Complaint: Photos, descriptions, AI severity. Device: Browser, OS, IP (for security). Usage: Features accessed, timestamps.

2. How We Use Your Data

To route complaints to the correct authority; send status updates; improve AI accuracy using anonymised data; generate anonymised road quality reports; verify identity; comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Sharing

We share data only with relevant government authorities for complaint resolution, or when legally required by court order. We never sell your data to advertisers, brokers, or commercial third parties.

4. Data Retention

Account data retained while active plus 12 months. Resolved complaints retained 7 years for government audit compliance. Request deletion at [email protected].

5. Security

All data transmitted via HTTPS/TLS 1.3. Passwords stored as PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes — we cannot read them. Regular security audits conducted. Breach notification within 72 hours.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to: access your data; correct inaccuracies; request erasure; withdraw consent; lodge a complaint with India's Data Protection Authority.

7. Cookies

Only essential authentication cookies (JWT session token) are used. No tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies.

8. Third-Party Services

OpenStreetMap for maps (no personal data shared). Gmail SMTP for verification emails (email address only). No other third parties access your data.

9. Children's Privacy

SADAK AI is not for users under 18. We delete accounts of minors immediately upon discovery.
